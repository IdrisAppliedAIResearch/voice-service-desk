title: Guest Wi-Fi for visitors and vendors
category: Network
tags: [wifi, guest, visitors]

The guest wifi for visitors and vendors is CH-Guest. It needs approval from a Contoso Health staff sponsor, lasts 24 hours, and gives internet access only.

For the guest:
1. Open the Wi-Fi list on your device and choose CH-Guest.
2. A registration page opens in your browser. If it does not appear, open any website and you will be sent to it.
3. Enter your name, your mobile number or email address, and the work email address of the Contoso Health employee you are visiting. That employee is your sponsor.
4. Keep the page open while your sponsor approves. The page confirms when you are on the wifi.

For the sponsor:
1. You get an email asking you to approve the guest by name.
2. Approve only people you know and are expecting. If you do not recognize the name, decline the request.
3. If the email does not arrive, check your junk folder, then ask the guest to register again with your exact work email address.

Access lasts 24 hours from approval. After that, the guest registers again and the sponsor approves again, so a vendor on site for a week needs a fresh approval each day.

CH-Guest reaches the internet only. Guests cannot reach Contoso Health systems, shared drives, or printers. A vendor who needs to work on internal systems should ask their Contoso Health contact to open a ticket, rather than relying on guest Wi-Fi.

Staff should not put Contoso Health laptops on CH-Guest. Managed devices use CH-Secure, which they join automatically.

Nobody, guest or staff, may join CH-Clinical. That network is reserved for medical devices.

Still stuck? Sponsors can call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 for help with guest access.
