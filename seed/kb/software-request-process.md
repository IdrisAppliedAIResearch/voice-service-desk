To get software that is not already in Company Portal or Self Service, submit a Request Software form in the IT Self-Service Portal and collect the approvals it needs.

Contoso Health computers do not give staff administrator rights, so software is installed only through approved channels. First check whether you need a request at all. Pre-approved software installs from Company Portal on Windows or Self Service on a Mac, with no ticket or approval.

To submit a request:
1. Go to itportal.contoso-health.example and select Request Software.
2. Enter the software name, the vendor, the version if it matters, and your computer name. On Windows, the computer name is under Settings, System, About. On a Mac, it is under System Settings, General, About.
3. Give the business reason in a sentence or two, such as the task or project that needs it.
4. Say whether the software is free or licensed. For licensed software, enter the cost center that will pay for the license.
5. Submit the form. Your manager gets an approval email.

Every request needs your manager's approval. Licensed software also needs cost-center approval from the owner of that budget, because the license is charged to it. Software that Contoso Health has never used before also gets a security review before it is approved, which can add a few days.

Track the request under My Requests in the portal. You get an email at each step. Once every approval is in, the software is either installed on your computer automatically or added to Company Portal or Self Service for you to install. Installation is handled as a P4 request, which has a response target of 3 business days.

If a request is declined, the email explains why and often names an approved alternative that does the same job.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 and have your request number ready.
