Remote Desktop lets you use your Windows office computer while you are away from the office, and it works only while your Contoso Health laptop is on the VPN.

Before you leave the office:
1. Find your office computer's name. On that computer, open Settings, System, About, and write down the Device name.
2. Leave the computer turned on. Locking it is fine, but do not shut it down. A computer that is off or asleep cannot be reached, and nobody can turn it on for you remotely.

To use it remotely:
1. On your Contoso Health laptop, sign in to the VPN and wait for it to show Connected.
2. On Windows, open the Start menu, type Remote Desktop, and open the app. On a Contoso Health Mac, install Microsoft Remote Desktop from Self Service first, then add your office computer as a PC.
3. Enter the office computer's name and start the session.
4. Sign in with your work email address and password.
5. Your office desktop opens in a window, with your programs and files as you left them.

When you are done for the day, sign out from the Start menu on the remote computer. Closing the window only disconnects the session and leaves your programs running. Never choose Shut down on the remote computer, because it stays off until someone at the office turns it on.

Common problems:
If Remote Desktop cannot find the computer, check that the VPN is on and that the name is spelled correctly. If it still fails, the office computer is probably off or asleep.
If a message says you are not allowed to sign in remotely, your account does not have remote access to that computer yet. Ask the service desk to check it.

Remote Desktop needs a Contoso Health laptop with the VPN installed. On a personal computer, use CH Remote Apps at remote.contoso-health.example instead. It runs in a web browser and does not need the VPN.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 and give them your office computer's name.
