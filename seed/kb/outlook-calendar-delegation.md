title: Give someone access to your Outlook calendar
category: Email
tags: [outlook, calendar, delegate]
updated: 2024-06-17

You can share your Outlook calendar with a coworker, or make them a delegate who handles meeting requests for you, without contacting the service desk.

In classic Outlook for Windows:

1. Open your calendar, and on the Home tab select Share Calendar, then Calendar.
2. Select Add, find the person by name, and select OK.
3. Choose a permission level and select OK.

In the new Outlook or Outlook on the web, right-click your calendar, select Sharing and permissions, type the person's name, choose a permission level, and select Share. In Outlook for Mac, right-click the calendar, select Sharing Permissions, and add the person.

Pick the lowest level that does the job. Can view when I'm busy shows only free and busy times. Can view titles and locations adds meeting names and rooms. Can view all details shows everything except private items. Can edit lets the person create and change items. Delegate also lets them receive and respond to meeting requests on your behalf.

When you add a delegate, choose whether meeting requests go to your delegate only or to both of you. Sending them to both of you means you still see every request.

To hide an appointment, even from people who can view all details, open it and select Private. Delegates cannot see your private items unless you allow it.

The person you share with gets an email invitation. Once they select Accept, your calendar appears under Shared calendars in their Outlook, which can take a few minutes.

To stop sharing, return to the same permissions screen and remove the person, or change their level.
