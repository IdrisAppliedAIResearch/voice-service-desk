title: Request building or room badge access
category: Facilities
tags: [badge, access, facilities]
updated: 2024-07-15

To have your ID badge open a building, unit, or room it does not open yet, submit a Badge Access request in the IT Self-Service Portal, which your manager and the area owner must both approve.

1. Go to itportal.contoso-health.example and choose Badge Access.
2. Pick the building and the door, unit, or room. If you are not sure of its name, describe where it is, such as the third floor supply room in Building A.
3. Explain why you need access, and choose permanent access or temporary access with an end date.
4. Submit the request. Your manager approves it first, and then the area owner approves it. The area owner is the leader responsible for that space, such as the pharmacy manager for a pharmacy.

Restricted areas such as pharmacies, medication rooms, and data centers are approved only when your job requires them. The area owner may ask for more detail or decline the request.

After the final approval, Security Operations adds the access to your badge, usually within one business day, and you get an email when it is active. Tap your badge on the reader to test it. If the light stays red, call Security Operations at extension 2200.

Managers can request access for a new team member the same way, as soon as that person's account exists.

Using your badge:
Tap your own badge at every secured door, even when someone holds it open. Do not let anyone follow you through, never lend your badge, and never prop a door open. If someone you do not know asks you to let them in, send them to the nearest front desk.

Problems with doors or readers, such as a reader that does not respond or a door that will not lock, go to Security Operations at extension 2200 rather than the service desk.

Still stuck? For help with the Badge Access form or its approvals, call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100. For doors and badges, call Security Operations at extension 2200.
