If you forgot your password or your account is locked, you can reset it yourself at passwordreset.contoso-health.example, as long as Microsoft Authenticator is already set up on your phone.

1. In any web browser, on a computer or a phone, go to passwordreset.contoso-health.example. You can also select Forgot my password on the Contoso Health sign-in page.
2. Enter your work email address and type the characters shown in the picture.
3. Approve the Microsoft Authenticator request on your phone by typing the number shown on the screen.
4. Choose a new passphrase. It must be at least 14 characters and cannot match any of your last 24 passwords.
5. Select Finish. The new password works right away, and a locked account is unlocked.
6. Sign in again with the new password on every device that used the old one, including your phone's email app, so those devices do not lock your account.

If your Windows laptop is off site, the sign-in screen may accept only the old password at first. Sign in with the old password, connect to the VPN, then lock the screen and unlock it with the new password.

If you never set up Microsoft Authenticator, or you lost the phone it was on, self-service reset cannot confirm it is you, so call the service desk instead. The service desk verifies your identity with your security questions, then sends a temporary password to the email address and mobile phone on file. You must change the temporary password the next time you sign in.

The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
