If a ticket is not moving or an issue has become more urgent, call the service desk with your ticket number and ask for the shift lead.

1. Find your ticket number. It is in your confirmation email and under My Requests in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example.
2. Call extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from outside. The phone line is staffed 24 hours a day, so you can escalate at any hour.
3. Give your ticket number and ask for the shift lead.
4. Explain what has changed. For example, more people are affected, patient care is now involved, a workaround stopped working, or a deadline is close.
5. The shift lead reviews the ticket and can raise its priority, move it to a specialist team, or get you an update from the technician working on it.

Critical issues must be phoned in:
P1 issues, where patient care is affected or many users are down with no workaround, must be reported by phone and never by email. A phone call puts a person on the problem right away, which is how the 15-minute response target for P1 is met. If you already sent an email about something critical, call as well.

Tips for a faster escalation:
Add information to your existing ticket instead of opening a new one. Duplicate tickets split the history and slow the work down.
Be specific about the impact. For example, say that twelve nurses on the fourth floor cannot sign in, rather than saying nothing works.
If you are escalating for a coworker, have their name and ticket number ready.

If you are unhappy with how a closed ticket was handled, call the service desk and ask the shift lead to review it.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 at any hour and ask for the shift lead.
