Android phones are enrolled with a work profile, a separate space on the phone that keeps Contoso Health apps and data apart from your personal apps, and you set it up with the Intune Company Portal app.

Before you start, set a screen lock, install the latest system update, and make sure you can approve Microsoft Authenticator prompts.

1. Install Intune Company Portal from the Google Play Store.
2. Open it and sign in with your Contoso Health email address and password. Approve the sign-in in Microsoft Authenticator by entering the number shown in Company Portal.
3. Tap Begin, review what Contoso Health can and cannot see, and tap Continue.
4. Tap Accept and continue to create the profile. This takes a few minutes, so do not close the app.
5. Company Portal then checks your device settings. Setup is complete when it shows that your device is compliant.

Using the profile:
Contoso Health apps have a small briefcase badge and appear under the Work tab in your app list. Outlook and Teams install automatically. To add other approved apps, open the Play Store that has the briefcase badge.
You can pause the Contoso Health apps when you are off shift with the pause switch on that tab. Their notifications stop until you turn them back on.
Keep Contoso Health files in Contoso Health apps. Copying data from them into personal apps may be blocked.

Privacy: Contoso Health manages only the profile. It cannot see your personal apps, photos, texts, call history, or browsing history.

If you remove the profile, or when you leave Contoso Health, only the profile and its data are deleted. Your personal apps and files stay on the phone.

If Company Portal says your phone is not compliant, tap its notification to see what to fix, usually a screen lock or a pending system update.
