title: Locked out of your account
category: Accounts

After 10 failed sign-in attempts, your Contoso Health account locks for 15 minutes, and you can either wait for it to unlock on its own or reset your password to unlock it right away.

While the account is locked, sign-in screens say your account is locked, even if you now type the correct password.

1. Stop trying to sign in. More attempts will not work while the account is locked.
2. Wait 15 minutes, then sign in once, carefully, with your current password.
3. If you do not remember your password, use self-service password reset at passwordreset.contoso-health.example. A successful reset unlocks your account right away. It needs Microsoft Authenticator set up on your phone.
4. If you cannot use self-service reset, call the service desk. After verifying your identity, the service desk can reset your password, which also unlocks the account.

If your account keeps locking after a password change, a device is still trying the old password. The usual causes are the email app on your phone or tablet, a Remote Desktop session left signed in on another computer, saved credentials in Windows Credential Manager or the Mac keychain, and a second computer you used recently. Update or remove the old password on each one, then wait 15 minutes before you sign in again.

If your account locks and you did not mistype your password, someone else may be trying to sign in as you. Tell the service desk, especially if you also get Authenticator requests you did not start.

The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
