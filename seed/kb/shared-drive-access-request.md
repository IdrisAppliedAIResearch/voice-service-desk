To get into a folder on a shared drive, submit an Access Request in the IT Self-Service Portal, and the folder's data owner approves it.

Your S: drive is your department share, under `\\files.contoso-health.example\shared\` with one folder per department. Your H: drive is your personal home drive, so you never need to request access to it.

1. Go to itportal.contoso-health.example and select Access Request.
2. Choose Shared drive folder as the request type.
3. Enter the full path of the folder. In File Explorer, right-click the folder and select Copy as path, then paste it into the form. If you cannot see the folder at all, ask a coworker who uses it to copy the path for you.
4. Choose Read only if you only need to open files, or Modify if you need to create, change, or delete them. Ask for the lowest access that lets you do your work.
5. Explain why you need access, and submit the request.

The request goes to the folder's data owner, usually a department manager, who approves or declines it. You get an email either way. The service desk cannot approve access for the data owner.

New access takes effect after you sign out of your computer and sign back in. Restarting also works. If you are working off site, connect to the VPN after you sign back in, then open the drive.

A manager can also submit the request for a team member, for example a new hire, by naming that person in the form.

If you have access but the S: drive itself is missing, see the article on mapping a shared drive.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line, or open a ticket at itportal.contoso-health.example.
