title: Fix Teams microphone, speaker, and camera problems
category: Collaboration
tags: [teams, audio, video, meetings]
updated: 2025-04-02
---
When your microphone, speaker, or camera is not working in Teams and people cannot hear or see you, the cause is usually the wrong device selected or a privacy setting that blocks Teams.

1. Check the hardware. Make sure your headset is connected, its mute switch is off, and your camera's privacy cover is open. Unplug a USB headset or camera and plug it back in.
2. Pick the right devices. In Teams, select the three dots next to your profile picture, then Settings, then Devices, and choose your speaker, microphone, and camera.
3. Make a test call. On the same Devices page, select Make a test call to record a short message and hear it played back.
4. In a meeting, select More, then Settings, then Device settings to switch devices without leaving the call.
5. Allow Teams to use the microphone and camera. On Windows, open Settings, then Privacy and security, and allow desktop apps under both Microphone and Camera. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and turn on Microsoft Teams under Microphone and Camera. Restart Teams afterward.
6. Close other apps that may be using the camera or microphone, such as another meeting app, then restart Teams.

If your audio is choppy or your video freezes, turn off your camera, pause large downloads, and move closer to your Wi-Fi router or use a wired network connection.

If Teams still cannot find your camera or microphone after a restart, select the three dots, then Check for updates, and restart your computer once the update finishes.
