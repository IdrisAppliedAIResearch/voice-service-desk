Pre-approved software installs from Company Portal on Windows or from Self Service on a Mac, with no ticket and no approval needed.

The apps in these catalogs are already licensed and tested for Contoso Health computers, so anyone can install them. Keep your computer plugged in and online while an app installs.

On a Windows computer:
1. Open the Start menu, type Company Portal, and open the app.
2. Select Apps to browse the catalog, or type the app name in the search box.
3. Select the app, then select Install. The status changes from Downloading to Installing to Installed.
4. If the app asks for a restart, save your work first.

On a Mac:
1. Open Self Service from the Applications folder or the Dock.
2. Browse the categories, or search for the app by name.
3. Click Install. A progress bar appears under the app, and a notification tells you when it is done.
4. Open the app from the Applications folder.

Apps installed this way are kept up to date automatically. If one stops working, installing it again from Company Portal or Self Service usually repairs it.

If an install fails, restart the computer, make sure at least 10 GB of disk space is free, and try once more. If it still fails, report it to the service desk with the app name and the exact error message.

If the software you need is not in the catalog, you cannot install it yourself, because staff accounts do not have administrator rights. Submit a Request Software form in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example instead. Your manager approves the request, and licensed software also needs cost-center approval.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, or open a ticket in the IT Self-Service Portal.
