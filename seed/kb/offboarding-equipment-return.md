---
title: Return equipment when someone leaves
category: Devices
tags: [offboarding, equipment, return]
updated: 2024-12-02
---
When an employee leaves Contoso Health, their laptop, phone, and accessories go back to the IT Bar or in a prepaid return box.

Return everything IT issued: the laptop and charger, any docking station, monitor, keyboard, mouse, or headset, and any Contoso Health phone or tablet.

Before returning a device:
1. Copy personal files, such as family photos, to your own storage. Do not copy Contoso Health or patient information.
2. Move work files the team still needs to the department's S: drive.
3. Do not reset or wipe the device, and do not remove the asset tag. IT wipes every device after checking it in.
4. If you signed a Contoso Health iPhone or iPad into a personal Apple Account, sign out of it first, or the device cannot be reused.

On site:
Bring everything to the IT Bar on the first floor of Building A, Main Campus, on or before the last day. A technician checks in each item and emails a receipt to the employee's manager.

Remote employees:
The manager asks for a prepaid return box in the Offboarding Request in the IT Self-Service Portal. The box arrives at the employee's address with a prepaid shipping label. Pack the items, seal the box, attach the label, and drop it off with the carrier within 5 business days of receiving it. Keep the tracking number until IT confirms the box arrived.

Missing or damaged equipment:
Tell the manager and the service desk right away. A missing laptop or phone is locked and wiped remotely to protect patient information, so report it as soon as you notice.

ID badges are not IT equipment. Return your badge to the Security office on the ground floor of Building A, or put it in the return box if you work remotely.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 for help with returns.
