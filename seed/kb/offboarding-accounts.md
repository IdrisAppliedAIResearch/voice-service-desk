When someone leaves Contoso Health, their manager submits an Offboarding Request in the IT Self-Service Portal at least 5 business days before the last day, and the accounts are disabled at 5 PM on that last day.

To submit the request:
1. Go to itportal.contoso-health.example and choose Offboarding Request.
2. Enter the employee's name, employee ID, last day, and whether they work on site or remotely.
3. Name the person taking over their work. If the employee is the data owner for a shared drive folder, name the new data owner so access requests keep getting approved.
4. Say whether you need access to their mailbox, or an automatic reply that points senders to a new contact.
5. Submit it at least 5 business days ahead so equipment return and access changes can be scheduled.

What happens on the last day:
At 5 PM, the network account is disabled. That ends email, Teams, VPN, CH Remote Apps, shared drives, and clinical system access. Building badge access ends at the same time.

The mailbox is kept for 30 days after the last day. During that time, the manager can ask for access to it, or for an automatic reply, through the IT Self-Service Portal. After 30 days the mailbox is deleted and cannot be recovered.

Before leaving, the employee should move files the team still needs from their H: drive to the department's S: drive.

Urgent or involuntary departures:
If access must end immediately, HR or the manager should call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) rather than wait for the portal request. The service desk disables the account right away, and the Offboarding Request can be completed afterward.

Laptops, phones, and other equipment go back to the IT Bar or in a prepaid return box. The equipment return article has the details.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100.
