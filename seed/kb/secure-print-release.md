title: Release print jobs with your badge
category: Printing

Print jobs sent to the FollowMe queue wait on the print server until you tap your badge at a Contoso Health printer, so sensitive pages never sit in an output tray.

1. Print to FollowMe from any application. If FollowMe is not in your printer list, see the articles on adding a printer on Windows or on a Mac.
2. Go to any Contoso Health printer or copier with a badge reader, in any building.
3. Tap your ID badge flat on the reader and hold it there for a second.
4. Your waiting jobs appear on the printer's screen. Select Print All, or choose individual jobs and select Print.
5. Collect your pages, then select Log Out on the screen or tap your badge again.

The first time you use FollowMe, the printer may ask you to link your badge. Sign in on the printer's screen once with your Contoso Health username and password, and the printer remembers your badge after that.

Jobs you do not release within 24 hours are deleted automatically, so print again if you miss that window. You can also delete jobs you no longer need from the printer's screen.

If the reader beeps but no jobs appear, check that you printed to FollowMe and not to another printer, and that you are signed in to your computer as yourself. If the reader does not respond at all, try another printer and report the faulty one to the service desk. If your badge does not open doors either, contact Security Operations at extension 2200.

When you print patient information, release it right away and do not leave pages at the printer.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line, or open a ticket at itportal.contoso-health.example.
