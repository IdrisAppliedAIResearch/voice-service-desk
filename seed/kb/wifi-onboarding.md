The staff wifi is CH-Secure. Contoso Health laptops, phones, and tablets connect to it automatically, using a certificate that is installed on the device for you.

There is no wifi password to type. Every managed Contoso Health device gets a certificate that proves it belongs to Contoso Health, and CH-Secure accepts it.

To get on the wifi, join CH-Secure:
1. Open the Wi-Fi list. On Windows, select the network icon in the taskbar. On a Mac, select the Wi-Fi icon in the menu bar. On a phone, open Wi-Fi settings.
2. Choose CH-Secure. On a laptop, tick the box to join automatically next time.
3. If it joins without asking for anything, you are on the wifi.

If the wifi keeps dropping or stops working on a Contoso Health laptop or phone, or CH-Secure asks for a username, a password, or a certificate, cancel any prompt and do not type your password. Usually the device certificate is missing or expired. To fix it:
1. Give the device another way online, such as the network port at your desk for a laptop, or cellular data for a phone.
2. Restart the device and leave it online for 15 minutes so the certificate can renew. On Windows you can speed this up in Company Portal under Settings, then Sync.
3. Choose CH-Secure again, and the wifi should work.

Two other wifi networks appear on campus, and staff devices do not belong on either one.

CH-Guest is for visitors and vendors. It needs a sponsor's approval, lasts 24 hours, and reaches the internet only.

CH-Clinical is reserved for medical devices such as infusion pumps and patient monitors. Staff must never join it, even when it shows up in the list.

A personal phone or tablet can join CH-Secure only after you enroll it in Company Portal, which installs the certificate the same way. A personal device that is not enrolled cannot join.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 and tell them your device, your building, and the exact message you see.
