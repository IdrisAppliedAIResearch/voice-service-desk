title: Lost or damaged ID badge
category: Facilities
tags: [badge, lost, security]
updated: 2024-07-15
---
If your ID badge is lost or stolen, call Security Operations at extension 2200 right away so it can be deactivated, then get a replacement at the Security office on the ground floor of Building A.

1. Call Security Operations at extension 2200 as soon as you notice the badge is missing, even if you think it may turn up.
2. Security deactivates the badge immediately, so nobody can use it to open doors.
3. Go to the Security office on the ground floor of Building A, Main Campus, and bring a photo ID. Your replacement badge carries the same door access as the one it replaces.
4. If the missing badge turns up later, bring it to the Security office. It stays deactivated and cannot be used again.

Damaged or worn badge:
If your badge is cracked, the photo has faded, or readers no longer respond to it, bring it to the Security office for a replacement. They collect the damaged badge and deactivate it when they hand you the new one.

Until you have a replacement, do not borrow a coworker's badge or ask people to let you through secured doors. Check in at your building's front desk, or call Security Operations at extension 2200 if you cannot reach your work area.

If your phone went missing along with your badge, also call the service desk. After verifying your identity, the service desk removes the missing device from your Microsoft Authenticator sign-in methods and issues a temporary access pass, so you can sign in and set up Authenticator again.

Access to additional doors or rooms is a separate request. Use Badge Access in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example.

Still stuck? Call Security Operations at extension 2200 for anything about your badge, or the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100 for help signing in.
