The Contoso Health service desk answers the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, offers chat from 6 AM to 10 PM Eastern every day, and runs a walk-up IT Bar on weekdays.

Phone, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week:
Dial extension 4357 (H E L P) from any Contoso Health phone, or 555-0100 from outside. The phone line is staffed at all hours, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Call for anything urgent, and always call for problems that affect patient care.

Chat, 6 AM to 10 PM Eastern every day:
Open the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example and select Chat with IT. Chat works well for how-to questions and for problems that are not urgent.

IT Bar, weekdays 8 AM to 5 PM:
Walk up to the IT Bar on the first floor of Building A, Main Campus, for laptop and phone problems, new hire sign-in, and equipment returns. No appointment is needed.

IT Self-Service Portal, any time:
Use itportal.contoso-health.example to open tickets, check their status, and submit software requests, access requests, badge access requests, and offboarding requests.

Self-service password reset, any time:
Reset a forgotten password at passwordreset.contoso-health.example. You need multi-factor authentication registered first.

Critical issues, priority P1, must be reported by phone. Do not use chat, email, or the portal for them, because a phone call puts a technician on the problem right away.

When you contact the service desk, have ready your name, your employee ID or work email address, your ticket number if you have one, your computer name, and the exact wording of any error message.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, any time of the day or night.
