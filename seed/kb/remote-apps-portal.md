CH Remote Apps lets you open Contoso Health applications or a full virtual desktop in a web browser on any computer, with no VPN needed.

It is the simplest option on a personal computer or a shared device, because nothing has to be installed. If you need your own office computer instead, use Remote Desktop from a Contoso Health laptop, which needs the VPN.

To start a session:
1. Open a current version of Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.
2. Go to remote.contoso-health.example.
3. Sign in with your work email address and password.
4. Approve the sign-in in Microsoft Authenticator by entering the number shown on your computer screen.
5. Choose an application, or choose CH Virtual Desktop for a full Windows desktop. It opens in a new browser tab.

While you work:
Your S: and H: drives are available inside the virtual desktop, so save your work there. Files saved to the virtual desktop itself are not kept after you sign out.
To protect patient information, you cannot download files to the computer you are using, and copying text out of a session is turned off.
Sessions that sit idle for 30 minutes are disconnected, and unsaved work can be lost, so save often.

When you finish, sign out from the Start menu inside the virtual desktop, then close the tab. Closing the tab without signing out leaves your session running until it times out.

If the page will not load, try a different browser or clear your browser's cache. If you can sign in but an application will not open, write down the application name and the error message before you call.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, or open a ticket in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example.
