On your first day, sign in at the IT Bar or with your one-time setup link, then set up multi-factor authentication before anything else.

1. Choose how to sign in. Visit the IT Bar on the first floor of Building A, Main Campus, and bring a photo ID. Or open the one-time setup link sent to the personal email address you gave HR. The link works only once, so do not forward it.
2. Create your password when prompted. The setup page lists the password requirements.
3. Enroll in multi-factor authentication that same day. Install Microsoft Authenticator from your phone's app store. On a computer, go to mysignins.contoso-health.example, choose Security info, then Add sign-in method, and pick Microsoft Authenticator. Scan the QR code with the app, then approve the test notification by entering the number shown on the screen.
4. Sign in to your Contoso Health laptop on campus. The first sign-in can take about 15 minutes while your apps and settings load, so keep it plugged in.
5. Open Outlook and Teams to check that email and chat work. Your S: drive is your department share and your H: drive is your personal home drive. Both appear in File Explorer.
6. To print, send jobs to the FollowMe queue, then release them by tapping your badge at any printer within 24 hours.

Once Authenticator is registered, you can reset a forgotten password yourself at passwordreset.contoso-health.example. Self-service reset does not work until multi-factor authentication is set up, which is why step 3 cannot wait.

More approved software is in Company Portal on Windows or Self Service on a Mac. Your manager can request anything else in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example.

Never share your password or an MFA code with anyone. The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, or stop by the IT Bar.
