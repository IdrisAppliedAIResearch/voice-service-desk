The Contoso Health service desk answers phone calls and chats Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern.

How to reach the service desk:
1. Phone: dial extension 4357 (H E L P) from any Contoso Health phone, or 555-0100 from outside.
2. Chat: open the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example and select Chat with IT.
3. Portal: submit a ticket or request in the IT Self-Service Portal at any hour.

Phone and chat are not staffed after 7 PM, before 7 AM, on weekends, or on Contoso Health holidays. During those times chat is unavailable and calls go to voicemail. Leave your name, a callback number, your ticket number if you have one, and a short description of the problem. Voicemails and tickets that arrive after hours are worked starting at 7 AM the next business day.

Urgent clinical issues after hours:
If a problem is affecting patient care and cannot wait for the next business day, such as a system outage on a nursing unit, call extension 4357 and choose the urgent clinical option in the phone menu. The on-call technician is paged and calls you back. Please use this option only for issues that affect patient care. Everything else is handled when the service desk opens.

You can reset a forgotten password yourself at any hour at passwordreset.contoso-health.example, as long as you have registered for multi-factor authentication.

When you contact the service desk, have this ready:
1. Your name and your employee ID or work email address.
2. Your computer name, if the problem is with a computer.
3. Your ticket number, if you already have one.
4. The exact wording of any error message.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, Monday through Friday from 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern.
