---
title: "Lost or replaced phone: restore multifactor authentication"
category: Security
tags: [mfa, lost-device, authenticator]
updated: 2025-03-10
---
If the phone you use for Microsoft Authenticator is lost, stolen, or replaced, move your multifactor authentication to a new phone so you can keep signing in.

If you still have the old phone and it works, move Authenticator yourself:

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on the new phone.
2. On your computer, go to mysignins.contoso-health.example and sign in, approving the request on the old phone.
3. Select Security info, then Add sign-in method, choose Authenticator app, and scan the QR code with the new phone.
4. Approve the test request on the new phone.
5. Delete the old phone from the Security info list, then remove your work account from Authenticator on the old phone.

If the old phone is lost, stolen, or already wiped, you cannot approve sign-ins, so call the service desk:

1. Call extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
2. The analyst verifies your identity before making any change.
3. The analyst removes the old Authenticator registration, so the lost phone can no longer approve sign-ins to your account.
4. The analyst issues you a temporary access pass, a one-time passcode that lets you sign in without your phone. It expires one hour after it is issued.
5. Go to mysignins.contoso-health.example, sign in with your work email address and the temporary access pass, and set up Authenticator on the new phone.

If the lost phone was enrolled with the Intune Company Portal app, tell the analyst so the work data on it can be removed remotely.

Never read an Authenticator number or a code to someone who calls you. The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
