title: Set up multifactor authentication (MFA) with Microsoft Authenticator

Contoso Health protects your account with Microsoft Authenticator push notifications and number matching, and you need to set this up before you can reset your own password.

You need a smartphone and a computer you can sign in to. New hires set up Authenticator on their first day.

1. On your phone, install Microsoft Authenticator from the App Store or Google Play. It is free.
2. On your computer, go to mysignins.contoso-health.example and sign in with your work email address and password.
3. Select Security info, then Add sign-in method, and choose Authenticator app.
4. Select Next until a QR code appears on the screen.
5. In the Authenticator app, tap the plus sign, choose Work or school account, then Scan a QR code, and scan the code on your computer screen.
6. The page sends a test request to your phone. Type the number shown on your computer screen into the app and tap Yes.
7. When the page says the notification was approved, you are done.

From now on, when you sign in to a Contoso Health app on a new device or after some time away, your screen shows a two-digit number. Open the request on your phone, type that number, and approve it. Allow notifications for Authenticator so requests appear right away.

Only approve requests you started yourself. If a request appears when you are not signing in, deny it, choose the option saying it was not you, and call the service desk, because someone may know your password. The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request.

Getting a new phone? Add it at mysignins.contoso-health.example before you wipe or trade in the old one.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
