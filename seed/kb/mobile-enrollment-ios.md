To use Contoso Health email, Teams, and other work apps on an iPhone or iPad, enroll the device in Intune with the Company Portal app.

iPhones and iPads issued by Contoso Health enroll automatically during first setup. Use these steps to set up a personal iPhone or iPad, or an issued device that was erased.

Before you start, set a device passcode, update your iPhone or iPad to the latest version of iOS or iPadOS, and make sure you can approve Microsoft Authenticator prompts.

1. Install Intune Company Portal from the App Store.
2. Open it and sign in with your work email address and password. Approve the sign-in in Microsoft Authenticator by entering the number shown in Company Portal.
3. Tap Begin, read what Contoso Health can and cannot see on your device, and tap Continue.
4. Tap Download to get the management profile, then tap Allow.
5. Open the Settings app and tap Profile Downloaded near the top, or go to General, then VPN and Device Management. Tap the Contoso Health profile, tap Install, enter your passcode, and tap Install again.
6. Go back to Company Portal. It checks your device settings, which can take a few minutes. Setup is complete when it shows a green check mark.
7. Outlook and Teams install automatically. Other approved apps are under Apps in Company Portal.

Privacy: Contoso Health can see your device model, your iOS version, and the apps it manages. It cannot see your photos, messages, call history, browsing history, or personal apps.

If you remove the management profile, or when you leave Contoso Health, the Contoso Health apps and their data are removed. Your personal content is not touched.

If Company Portal says the device is not compliant, open Company Portal and tap the device name to see what to fix, such as a missing passcode or an iOS update.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P) or 555-0100, or open a ticket in the IT Self-Service Portal at itportal.contoso-health.example.
