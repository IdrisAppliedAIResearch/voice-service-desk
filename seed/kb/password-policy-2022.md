Your Contoso Health network password signs you in to your computer, email, and most clinical and business applications, and it must meet the requirements below.

Every password must:

1. Be at least 8 characters long.
2. Include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one symbol, such as an exclamation point or a pound sign.
3. Not contain your name or your username.

Passwords expire every 90 days. Starting 14 days before yours expires, Windows and Outlook remind you to change it. If it does expire, you are asked to change it the next time you sign in to a Contoso Health computer.

To change your password on a Windows computer, press Control, Alt, and Delete together, select Change a password, type your old password, then type and confirm the new one. After you change it, update the password in your phone's email app and on any other device that signs in with it.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, your account locks for 30 minutes. You can wait 30 minutes and try again, or call the service desk to unlock it sooner. A phone or tablet still using your old password is the most common reason an account locks right after a password change.

Keep your password private. Do not write it down, share it with coworkers, or use it for personal websites. The service desk will never ask for your password.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
