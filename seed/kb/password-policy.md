Contoso Health passwords are now passphrases of at least 14 characters, and they no longer expire on a schedule.

The rules:

1. Your passphrase must be at least 14 characters long. Spaces are allowed, so three or four unrelated words make one that is easy to remember.
2. Passwords no longer expire every 90 days. Change yours only if you think someone else knows it, or when you are prompted to change it at sign-in.
3. You cannot reuse any of your last 24 passwords.
4. After 10 failed sign-in attempts, your account locks for 15 minutes and then unlocks on its own.

Pick words that are not about you. Avoid your name, your username, family or pet names, the word Contoso, sports teams, and seasons or years. Do not use a line from a song, movie, or book, because attackers try those first. Never reuse your Contoso Health passphrase on a personal account.

To change your password, press Control, Alt, and Delete on a Windows computer and select Change a password, or use self-service password reset at passwordreset.contoso-health.example, which needs Microsoft Authenticator set up first. After any change, update the password in your phone's email app and on any other device that signs in with it, or those devices can lock your account.

Keep your passphrase private. The service desk will never ask for your password or for a code you did not request. If someone asks you for it, hang up and report the call to the service desk.

Still stuck? Call the service desk at extension 4357 (H E L P), or 555-0100 from an outside line.
